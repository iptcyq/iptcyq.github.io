## Simulation of the spread of a virus

**Made for Singapore Mathematical Society Essay Competition 2021**

- Our team of 3 had to write a report on "Mathematics and Pandemics" for [the competition](https://sgmathsociety.org/sms-essay-competition-2021/)
- We used this simulation to support our report on the formulas we developed.
- It is a _very_ simplistic simulation, where you can adjust values like: range of spread, probability of infection, time taken to recover
- And it got 1st Prize (yay)
- Find the simulation here: [Virus spread simulation](https://f15hygames.itch.io/spread-simulation)


(A couple of years after that, I updated it to v2.0 to make things clearer)
