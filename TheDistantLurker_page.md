## The Distant Lurker

**Made in under 2 weeks, for Game Off 2021**

- A short game where you social engineer people. 
- Hack into the secretive company with a handy kit of tools! (conveniently placed bugs, email, and social media)
- It comes with _some_ world-building! (if you ignore the plotholes)
- Play the game here: [The Distant Lurker](https://f15hygames.itch.io/the-distant-lurker)
