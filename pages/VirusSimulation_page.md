## Simulation of the spread of a virus

**Made for Singapore Mathematical Society Essay Competition 2021**

- Our team of 3 had to write a report on "Mathematics and Pandemics" for [the competition](https://sgmathsociety.org/sms-essay-competition-2021/).
- We used this simulation to support our report on the mathematical formulas we developed. (Ok it was mostly my teammates who did the math-ing, I just implemented their math.)
- It is a _very_ simplistic simulation, where you can adjust values like: 
    - range of spread, 
    - probability of infection, 
    - time taken to recover, etc
- We got 1st Prize and the paper was published on SMS's Mathematical Medley in 2022 :D

**Post-mortem**

- The original version explained nothing and was made solely to get the results we needed. Old version [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08d9kdys03o)
- A couple of years after that, I was bored one day and updated it to v2.0 to make things clearer.
