## Simulation of the spread of a virus

**Made for Singapore Mathematical Society Essay Competition 2021**

- Our team of 3 had to write a report on "Mathematics and Pandemics" for [the competition](https://sgmathsociety.org/sms-essay-competition-2021/).
- We used this simulation to support our report on the mathematical formulas we developed. (Ok it was mostly my teammates who did the math-ing, all I did is implement their math.)
- It is a _very_ simplistic simulation, where you can adjust values like: range of spread, probability of infection, time taken to recover, etc
- It got 1st Prize and was published on SMS's Mathematical Medley (Volume 48 Issue 1/2) :D
- Find the simulation here: [Virus spread simulation](https://f15hygames.itch.io/spread-simulation)

The original version explained nothing and was made solely to get the results we needed.

A couple of years after that, I updated it to v2.0 to make things clearer.
